Iron

Log the set. Skip the noise.

Iron is a workout tracker for Android. It logs every set you lift, suggests your next weight from your own history, and tracks food, water and bodyweight, so you can see whether your training is working.

What Iron does

Training: today's workout from your plan, set-by-set logging, a rest timer, warm-ups and personal bests.

Food and water: calories, protein and water against daily targets.

Body: a smoothed bodyweight trend, measurements and recovery check-ins.

Progress: weekly volume, lift trends, levels and badges.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your phone, encrypted. Signing in with Google is optional: it keeps an encrypted copy of your training log in a private folder in your own Google Drive, so you can get it back on a new phone. Iron has no server, no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
