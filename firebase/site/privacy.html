Iron

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

This policy explains what the Iron app for Android collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Iron is a personal training log made by an independent developer.

Data Iron keeps on your phone

What you enter: workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort, pain flags and notes), training plans, bodyweight and body measurements, daily check-ins (sleep, soreness and stress), food and water logs, your profile (name, age, sex, height and goals) and app settings.

It is stored in an encrypted database on your phone and used only to run the app: suggesting weights, working out calorie and water targets, and showing your progress.

Data Iron sends

None, unless you choose to sign in with Google. Iron has no server of its own. It has no analytics, no ads, no advertising identifier and no crash reporting, and it never sells data.

Google user data

What Iron accesses. If you sign in with Google, Iron receives your Google account's name and email address, and access to a private app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). Iron can see only the files it created in that folder, not any other file in your Drive.

How Iron uses it. Your name and email are shown in the app so you know which account is signed in, and are stored only on your phone. The app-data folder holds encrypted copies of your Iron data (backups): Iron writes them after a workout, when you put the app away with new data, and when you open it after a long gap, and reads one back when you restore, for example on a new phone.

Sharing. Iron does not share, transfer or sell Google user data. It is never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never read by people. There is no Iron server: data moves directly between your phone and your Google account.

Retention and deletion. Iron keeps the five most recent automatic backups and deletes older ones. Delete all data, in the app under You → Backup & data, erases everything on your phone and every backup in your Drive, then signs you out. You can also remove the backups in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → Iron → Delete hidden app data, and remove Iron's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use. Iron's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

The database on your phone is encrypted, and so is every backup. The key that opens a backup is stored with it in your Drive, so that signing in on a new phone brings everything back without a phrase to type. That means your backups are protected by your Google account: keep it secure.

Permissions

Notifications, for reminders you turn on and the rest timer, which runs as a foreground service so it keeps time with the screen off. Internet, only for Google sign-in and backups. Iron does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

Iron is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
